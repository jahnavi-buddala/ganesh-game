# Vighnaharta: model review, round 1

These are seven original, editable 3D model drafts based on the supplied visual reference. They are available as self-contained GLB files in the models folder and can be rotated in the review gallery.

- Mushika: silver-gray fur, pink ears and paws, amber eyes, a curved tail, and a red cape with raised gold Om decoration. Includes articulated Idle, Run, Jump, Slide and Land animation studies.
- Golden modak: fourteen pleated folds and a pointed tip.
- Lord Ganesha: seated on a lotus pedestal with a crown, halo, garland, blessing hand and offering. Intended to remain in the distance throughout the endless run.
- Festival tram: decorated vehicle with a flat roof for landing and running.
- Slide barrier: an overhead obstacle with clearance underneath.
- Temple rooftop: an elevated running surface.
- Roof ramp: a transition to the elevated route.

## Approval and limitations

These are first-round geometry and motion studies, not an exact reproduction of the reference illustration. The reference's cinematic fur, facial styling and lighting are not fully matched. Mushika uses animated articulated parts rather than a finished skinned character rig. The models need game optimization, animation refinement and collision integration after visual approval.

The model review is separate from the game. No new models or gameplay changes have been integrated as part of this review. Please review the mouse's face, ears, body proportions, cape and motion first, then the other six assets. Specify which models are approved and which details need revision before integration.

To reopen the viewer locally, run `node model-workbench/serve-review.mjs` from the game design folder and visit http://127.0.0.1:5180/.
